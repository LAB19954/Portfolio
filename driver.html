A LITTLE CONTEXT

Not exactly
a one-track mind.

Mechanical engineering is the main lane, but Pranjal has never been very good at staying in one lane. Web development started as a side quest and slowly became the kind of thing that turns an idle evening into a functioning website.

He likes F1 because there is always something to notice: a line through a corner, a strategy call, a tenth of a second hiding in plain sight. The same curiosity shows up in the things he builds. Try something, see where it breaks, fix it, then make it a little better.
